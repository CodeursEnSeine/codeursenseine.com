Nicolas Frizzarin is a Google Developer Expert (GDE) in Web Technologies and Angular, a Former Microsoft Most Valuable Professional (MVP), and a JetBrains Community Contributor. As a Senior Staff Engineer and Developer Advocate at Sfeir, he leverages his expertise to build powerful web applications, with a strong focus on performance and creating an exceptional user experience.

Nicolas is a passionate open-source contributor, notably as a core team member of the popular NG-ZORRO Angular UI library. He regularly shares his knowledge and insights on front-end technologies as a speaker at major conferences like Devoxx and We Are Developer, and through technical articles on Dev.to. His work is driven by a passion for creating high-quality digital products and a commitment to helping others grow
